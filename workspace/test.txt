Every six months, several students sit for the Combined Defence Services Examination or CDS. Many of them choose OTA as their first preference while filling the application form, hence, once they qualify the written exam, they are eligible to select their CDS OTA SSB dates 2026.
Being eligible for this entry means that if they also clear the SSB, medicals, and merit, they will join the Officers Training Academy (OTA) at Chennai. This is why it is called CDS OTA entry.
The official website of Indian Army, that is, joinindianarmy.nic.in released the CDS OTA SSB dates 2026 for men and women candidates who have qualified the written examination and also made the announcement public through online posts.
Now that the news is out, you must be excited to select your dates and put your months of preparation to the test. This is particularly true for freshers, who find the first SSB to be special.
